# 🛍️ Mall Customer Segmentation

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer characteristics and purchasing behavior to identify meaningful customer segments.

Using the **Mall Customers Dataset**, the project explores customer demographics such as age and annual income, along with their spending scores. Since the dataset does not contain a predefined target variable, **Unsupervised Machine Learning** was used to discover hidden customer groups.

The main clustering technique used in this project is **K-Means Clustering**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
* Identify patterns and relationships between customer characteristics.
* Segment customers based on their income and spending behavior.
* Determine the optimal number of customer segments.
* Generate business insights that can support targeted marketing strategies.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the **Mall Customers Dataset**.

### Main Features

| Feature        | Description                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| CustomerID     | Unique customer identifier            |
| Gender         | Customer gender                       |
| Age            | Customer age                          |
| Annual Income  | Annual income in thousands of dollars |
| Spending Score | Customer spending score from 1 to 100 |

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading

The dataset was loaded and inspected to understand its structure, columns, data types, and dimensions.

### 2. Data Cleaning

The dataset was checked for:

* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* Data type issues
* Potential outliers

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Several visualizations were created to understand customer behavior, including:

* Gender Distribution
* Age Distribution
* Annual Income Distribution
* Spending Score Distribution
* Age vs. Annual Income
* Annual Income vs. Spending Score
* Age vs. Spending Score
* Correlation Heatmap

### 4. Feature Selection

The main features selected for customer segmentation were:

* Annual Income
* Spending Score

These features provide a clear representation of customers' financial characteristics and purchasing behavior.

### 5. Feature Scaling

`StandardScaler` was used to standardize the selected features before applying K-Means.

Scaling is important because K-Means relies on distance calculations, and features with different scales can affect the clustering results.

### 6. Choosing the Optimal K

The **Elbow Method** was used to analyze the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS).

The **Silhouette Score** was also calculated to support the selection of the optimal number of clusters.

### 7. K-Means Clustering

K-Means was applied to group customers into segments with similar characteristics.

Each customer was assigned to a cluster based on their similarity to the cluster centroid.

### 8. Customer Segmentation

The resulting clusters were analyzed based on:

* Average Age
* Average Annual Income
* Average Spending Score
* Number of Customers

The clusters were then interpreted from a business perspective.

## 💡 Business Insights

The clustering analysis can help identify different types of customers, such as:

* **High-Value Customers**
  Customers with high income and high spending behavior.

* **Potential Customers**
  Customers with relatively high income but moderate spending.

* **Low-Engagement Customers**
  Customers with lower spending behavior who may require targeted campaigns.

* **High-Income / Low-Spending Customers**
  Customers who have strong purchasing potential but are currently less engaged.

> The exact characteristics and interpretation of each segment depend on the clustering results generated by the model.

## 🤖 Why Clustering?

There is no predefined target variable in the dataset that tells us which segment each customer belongs to.

Therefore, traditional supervised classification is not appropriate.

Instead, **Clustering** allows us to discover hidden patterns and naturally occurring customer groups within the data.

---

## 📌 Why K-Means?

K-Means was selected because it is:

* Simple and easy to interpret.
* Efficient for numerical datasets.
* Suitable for customer segmentation.
* Effective when the goal is to group similar observations.

---

## 📊 Evaluation

The clustering model was evaluated using:

### Elbow Method

Used to analyze WCSS and identify a suitable number of clusters.

### Silhouette Score

Used to evaluate how well-separated and internally consistent the clusters are.

A higher Silhouette Score generally indicates better-defined clusters.
